# Meal & Sugar — privacy policy and support

Published at https://utrendtek.github.io/meal-and-sugar-privacy/ (GitHub
Pages, `main`, root). The app links here from Settings and the paywall;
App Store Connect uses `/` as the Privacy Policy URL and `/support.html` as
the Support URL.

English is the original; Spanish and Vietnamese are translations. Every
statement must stay true to the app and server in `utrendtek/meal-and-sugar`
(what is sent, kept and for how long) and match `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and
the App Store privacy labels. Change the date at the top when the policy
changes.
